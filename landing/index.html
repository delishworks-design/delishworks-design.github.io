What's inside

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Small, calm tools that work together — built for real days, not ideal ones.

Cycle & predictions

Log a period start and Bloom learns your rhythm. Three starts are enough to trust the pattern.

Daily logging

Water, mood, sleep, energy, symptoms and meals — tap once, no forms to fight with.

A calm planner

Keep tasks, meals and notes in one place. Yesterday stays available; today stays simple.

Smart insights

Bloom Garden grows with your logs, turning scattered days into gentle, readable patterns.

Reading library

Save books for offline reading, track minutes and let a small garden grow beside your streak.

Partner sharing

Invite someone with an 8-character code. They see a read-only view — and nothing they shouldn't.

Sync when you want

Start offline. Add an account later to keep your own devices in step, with real-time updates.

Reminders & widgets

One simple reminders toggle, plus home-screen widgets for water, mood and tasks.

Dark mode

A warm, low-light theme for late-night logging — including the reader and calendar.
